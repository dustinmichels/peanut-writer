# Less, placed well

White space is not wasted space. It is what lets the eye find where a thought begins and ends. A crowded page asks the
reader to do that work; a calm one does it for them.

> Perfection is achieved not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away.

# Three habits

1. **Generous margins.** Give text room on every side. A narrow column is easier to follow than a wide one.
2. **One idea per paragraph.** Short paragraphs leave breathing room between thoughts.
3. **Fewer kinds of emphasis.** Pick one way to stress a word and use it sparingly.

# Measure

Line length matters more than font size. Most readers are most comfortable with lines of 50 to 75 characters
(@tbl:measure).

| Characters per line | Effect on reading               |
| :------------------ | :------------------------------ |
| Under 45            | Choppy; the eye jumps too often |
| 50 to 75            | Comfortable                     |
| Over 90             | Tiring; easy to lose the line   |

Table: Line length and readability {#tbl:measure}

# In practice

Before adding anything to a page, ask what it is for. If the answer is unclear, leave it out. The page will be quieter,
and the reader will thank you for it.
