# Executive summary

The third quarter was our strongest yet. Shipments grew 14 percent over the second quarter, on-time delivery reached a
record 97.2 percent, and the new Riverside warehouse came online two weeks ahead of schedule.

Costs rose more slowly than volume, so the cost per shipment fell for the third quarter in a row. The main risk for the
coming quarter is driver availability during the holiday peak.

# Key figures

| Measure               |    Q2 |    Q3 |  Change |
| :-------------------- | ----: | ----: | ------: |

| On-time delivery      | 95.8% | 97.2% | +1.4 pt |

Table: Performance compared with the previous quarter {#tbl:kpis}

# Highlights

- **Riverside warehouse.** Opened on August 18 and now handles a fifth of all regional volume.
- **Route planning.** The new routing software cut average daily mileage per truck by 9 percent.
- **Safety.** No lost-time incidents for the second consecutive quarter.

# Outlook

We expect volume to rise a further 20 percent in the fourth quarter. To meet it, we plan to hire 35 seasonal drivers by
mid-November and extend Riverside's hours to two shifts. @tbl:kpis gives the baseline we will report against.
