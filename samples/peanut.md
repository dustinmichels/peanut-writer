# Brown butter cookies

These are the ones that sell out first. Browning the butter takes five extra minutes and makes all the difference.

1. Melt the butter over medium heat until it smells nutty and turns golden.
2. Let it cool a little, then beat in both sugars and the eggs.
3. Fold in the flour, salt and baking soda, then the chocolate chips.
4. Chill the dough for at least an hour, then bake at 350°F for 11 minutes.

> Tip: pull them out while the centers still look slightly underdone. They firm up as they cool.
