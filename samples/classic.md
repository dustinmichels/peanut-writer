# Summary

The empty lot at 41 Elm Street has sat unused for six years. We propose turning it into a community garden with
twenty-four raised beds, a tool shed and a shared compost area, run by volunteers and open to every household on the
street.

# Why a garden

A garden gives neighbors a reason to meet. It also puts fresh vegetables within walking distance of families who
currently drive across town for them, and it replaces a patch of weeds and broken glass with something the street can be
proud of.

- **Food**: each bed can grow roughly 40 pounds of vegetables a season.
- **Community**: work days and a harvest supper bring people together.
- **Learning**: the elementary school has asked to use two beds for its science classes.

# Budget

Most of the cost is lumber and soil. The city's Green Spaces grant covers up to $5,000, which leaves a small amount for
us to raise ourselves (@tbl:budget).

# Next steps

If the association approves this proposal at its April meeting, we will apply for the grant in May, build the beds over
two weekends in June and plant the first crops by midsummer.
