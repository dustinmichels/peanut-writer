# Peanut

![Peanut, the mascot](/peanut.svg){width=120}

Peanut is your writing buddy: a little 3D peanut who keeps you company in the top bar while you write.

## What he does

- **Sits** on the edge of the ribbon tabs, legs dangling, left of the title bar buttons. He breathes and blinks.
- **Now and then** (every half minute or so) he stands up or sits back down, strolls along the title bar for a look
  around and comes back, walks a few steps and turns to show you his back, or puts on a cowboy hat or a knit party
  hat, and later takes it off.
- **Less often** he puts on headphones and jams, nodding and tapping along as notes float up; jumps for joy, with a
  twirl; or walks over to the Commands button for a closer look, hops up to poke it crooked, and pokes it straight
  again. His arms only come out for these.
- **Click him** for a word of encouragement: "One word at a time.", "Shell yeah! Keep writing!", "Done is better than
  perfect." The bubble fades after a few seconds, and he never says the same thing twice running.

He never speaks unless you click him, and clicks beside him reach whatever is underneath.

## Turning him down or off

Set **Peanut's activity level** in [Settings](./settings) to **Busy** and he's up to something every few seconds;
**Chill** (the default) keeps him calmer. Turn off **Show Peanut** to hide him. Both apply to every document or just
this one.

## Where he comes from

Peanut started as a standalone toy, [dustinmichels/peanut](https://github.com/dustinmichels/peanut). Visit him at
[dustinmichels.github.io/peanut](https://dustinmichels.github.io/peanut/), where he also yodels and grows a mustache.
